# International Undergraduate Program (IUP ITB)

The International Undergraduate Program (IUP) at Institut Teknologi Bandung provides an immersive global learning environment conducted entirely in English. Established to educate world-class engineers, scientists, and business leaders, IUP combines ITB's rigorous foundational curriculum with mandatory international exposure.

---

## 1. Overview and Program Highlights

- **Language of Instruction:** 100% English across all lectures, lab work, exams, and undergraduate theses.
- **Student Cohort:** Diverse community comprising Indonesian students and international exchange/degree-seeking students.
- **Global Network:** Collaborative degree pathways with premier research universities in Europe, Australia, and Asia.
- **Official Portal:** https://admission.itb.ac.id/iup

## 3. Mandatory International Exposure

Every IUP student is required to complete at least one international exposure module prior to graduation:
1. **Double Degree (3+1 or 2+2):** Students spend the final 1 to 2 years at an accredited overseas partner university and receive two bachelor degrees upon completion.
2. **Semester Exchange:** Enrolling in full-time accredited coursework abroad for 1 to 2 semesters.
3. **International Research & Industry Internship:** Undertaking scientific thesis research at partner laboratories or global corporate centers.

### Renowned Partner Universities
- **Europe:** TU Delft, University of Groningen, TU Darmstadt, KU Leuven, KTH Royal Institute of Technology.
- **Australia:** University of Queensland, Monash University, University of New South Wales (UNSW).
- **Asia:** Tokyo Institute of Technology, Kyoto University, National University of Singapore (NUS).

## 4. Admissions Criteria & Selection Procedure

Admissions are conducted in three admission intakes annually:
1. **Academic Qualification:** High school transcript evaluation emphasizing advanced mathematics, physics, and chemistry.
2. **English Language Proficiency Thresholds:**
   - TOEFL iBT: Minimum score of 80 (or TOEFL ITP min 550)
   - IELTS Academic: Minimum band of 6.5
   - Duolingo English Test (DET): Minimum score of 110
   - ITB English Language Proficiency Test (ELPT): Equivalent score
3. **ITB Academic Qualification Assessment Session (ITB AQAS):** Comprehensive assessment covering analytical mathematics, logical reasoning, and academic English writing.
4. **Personal Interview:** Conducted in English to evaluate academic commitment, critical reasoning, and global perspective.
