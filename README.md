## Bahasa Indonesia

Basis data terbuka dan portal direktori interaktif untuk seluruh program studi, unit penerimaan TPB (Tahap Persiapan Bersama), akreditasi nasional dan internasional, alokasi daya tampung (SNBP, SNBT, SM-ITB), kurikulum inti, serta prospek karir lulusan di **Institut Teknologi Bandung (ITB)**.

Direktori ini dirancang bagi calon mahasiswa baru, mahasiswa aktif TPB, sivitas akademika, dan peneliti pendidikan tinggi untuk mengeksplorasi ekosistem akademik sains, rekayasa, seni rupa, dan manajemen bisnis di tiga kampus utama ITB (Ganesa Bandung, Jatinangor Sumedang, dan Cirebon).

### Fitur Utama

- **Pencarian Cepat:** Pencarian cerdas secara instan berdasarkan nama program studi, kode prodi, singkatan fakultas, kata kunci kurikulum, maupun karir lulusan.
- **Dukungan Dwibahasa Penuh:** Beralih instan antara Bahasa Indonesia dan English dengan persistensi pengaturan lokal.
- **Filter Multi-Kategori Komprehensif:**
  - **Jenjang:** Sarjana (S1) dan Magister (S2).
  - **Fakultas / Sekolah (12 Unit):** FITB, FMIPA, FSRD, FTI, FTMD, FTSL, FTTM, SAPPK, SBM, SF, SITH, dan STEI.
  - **Rumpun Keilmuan:** Sains & Teknologi (Saintek), Seni & Desain, Bisnis & Manajemen, dan Sosial Humaniora (Soshum).
  - **Sebaran Kampus:** Kampus ITB Ganesa (Bandung), Kampus ITB Jatinangor (Sumedang), dan Kampus ITB Cirebon.
  - **Akreditasi Global:** ABET, ASIIN, AACSB, KAAB, dan IABEE.
- **Tinjauan Profil Interaktif (Modal View):**
  - Alokasi kuota daya tampung: Total kursi, SNBP Prestasi (30%), SNBT UTBK (40%), dan Seleksi Mandiri ITB (30%).
  - Penjelasan mekanisme jalur penerimaan dan peminatan Tahap Persiapan Bersama (TPB).
  - Deskripsi program studi dan fokus keilmuan inti.
  - Lintasan karir alumni di sektor industri global, riset terapan, dan kewirausahaan teknologi.
  - Referensi penetapan Uang Kuliah Tunggal (UKT 1 hingga UKT 5 / SPP Pascasarjana).
  - Tautan ke website resmi program studi dan tombol salin tautan permanen (permalink sharing).
- **Deep Linking:** Buka profil program studi tertentu langsung melalui parameter URL (`?prodi={id}`).
- **Mode Tampilan:** Pilihan tampilan visual kartu (Grid View) dan tabel ringkas (Table View) dengan pengurutan kolom.
- **Open Data & Zero Dependencies:** File data murni JSON, JS, CSV, dan HTML mandiri tanpa ketergantungan runtime eksternal.

### Mekanisme Tahap Persiapan Bersama (TPB)

Penerimaan jenjang Sarjana (S1) di ITB menerapkan sistem **Tahap Persiapan Bersama (TPB)** pada Tahun Pertama:
- Calon mahasiswa mendaftar ke unit **Fakultas atau Sekolah** (misalnya STEI-Komputasi, STEI-Rekayasa, FTI-Ganesa, FTTM-Ganesa, SAPPK-Ganesa, FMIPA-MIPA, FSRD, atau SBM), bukan langsung ke program studi spesifik.
- Selama tahun pertama (semester 1 dan 2), seluruh mahasiswa menempuh fondasi keilmuan matematika, fisika, kimia, dan komputasi dasar secara setara.
- Di akhir tahun pertama, mahasiswa memilih program studi tujuan berdasarkan minat, kapasitas daya tampung prodi, dan indeks prestasi kumulatif (IPK) TPB.
- Jalur peminatan khusus (Peminatan Mandiri) disediakan untuk beberapa program studi strategis dan program multidisiplin kampus Jatinangor serta Cirebon.

### Jalur Penerimaan Mahasiswa Baru

- **SNBP (Seleksi Nasional Berdasarkan Prestasi):** Seleksi berbasis nilai rapor dan portofolio prestasi (kuota ~30%).
- **SNBT (Seleksi Nasional Berdasarkan Tes):** Seleksi nasional ujian tertulis berbasis komputer / UTBK (kuota ~40%).
- **SM-ITB (Seleksi Mandiri ITB):** Seleksi ujian mandiri daring ITB, nilai UTBK, dan tes kemampuan akademik/seni rupa (kuota ~30%).
- **IUP (International Undergraduate Program):** Program sarjana kelas internasional dengan bahasa pengantar bahasa Inggris dan program kemitraan pertukaran luar negeri.

## English

An open dataset and interactive web directory cataloging all study programs, TPB (Common First Year) admission faculties, national and international accreditations, seat capacity allocations (SNBP, SNBT, SM-ITB), core curricula, and career pathways at **Bandung Institute of Technology (ITB)**.

Designed for prospective university applicants, active students, faculty members, and educational researchers to systematically explore Indonesia's top science, engineering, fine arts, and management institution across its three campuses: Ganesa (Bandung), Jatinangor (Sumedang), and Cirebon.

### Key Features

- **Instant Search:** Search across program titles, codes, faculty abbreviations, curriculum topics, and graduate career paths.
- **Complete Bilingual Interface:** Toggle instantly between Bahasa Indonesia and English with client-side persistence.
- **Multi-Category Filtering:**
  - **Degree Level:** Undergraduate (S1) and Master's (S2).
  - **Faculty / School (12 Units):** FITB, FMIPA, FSRD, FTI, FTMD, FTSL, FTTM, SAPPK, SBM, SF, SITH, and STEI.
  - **Academic Cluster:** Science & Technology, Fine Arts & Design, Business & Management, and Social Sciences.
  - **Campus Locations:** Ganesa Campus (Bandung), Jatinangor Campus (Sumedang), and Cirebon Campus.
  - **Global Accreditations:** ABET, ASIIN, AACSB, KAAB, and IABEE.
- **Detailed Program Profile Modal:**
  - Exact seat capacity breakdown: Total seats, SNBP (30%), SNBT (40%), and SM-ITB (30%).
  - Explanation of the Common First Year (TPB) faculty admission model.
  - Scientific overview, key learning outcomes, and core curriculum competencies.
  - Graduate career trajectories across high-tech industries, research labs, and tech entrepreneurship.
  - Tuition fee reference (UKT Category 1 to 5 / Graduate fees).
  - Direct links to official study program web portals and permalink sharing.
- **Deep Linking:** Access any study program profile directly via URL query parameter (`?prodi={id}`).
- **Dual Display Modes:** Flexible Grid View and sortable compact Table View.
- **Open Data Formats:** Available in JSON, JS, and CSV without external runtime dependencies.

### The TPB (Common First Year) System

Undergraduate admissions at ITB operate on the **Tahap Persiapan Bersama (TPB)** system:
- High school graduates are admitted into a **Faculty or School** unit (e.g. STEI-Computing, STEI-Engineering, FTI, FTTM, SAPPK, FMIPA, SBM, FSRD) rather than an individual major.
- During Year 1 (semesters 1 and 2), all students take foundational coursework in advanced calculus, physics, chemistry, and computational thinking.
- At the conclusion of Year 1, students are placed into their degree specialization according to their preference, academic performance during TPB, and program quotas.
- Specific programs at Jatinangor and Cirebon campuses also offer direct admission pathways.
